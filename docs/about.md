# Team and research

OpenDPD is developed by the [Lab of Efficient Machine Intelligence](https://www.tudemi.com/) at Delft University of Technology. It brings PA modeling, neural digital predistortion and reproducible evaluation into one open research framework.

## Research and citation

Start with the [OpenDPD framework paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCAS58744.2024.10558162). The [citation guide](community/citation.md) contains BibTeX, related papers and how to cite software, data and leaderboard entries. GitHub's **Cite this repository** button uses `CITATION.cff` at the repository root.

[Watch the measured OpenDPDv2 demo](https://youtu.be/n3765lm3QZI), then see the [feature history](whats-new.md), [benchmark](benchmark/index.md) and [reproduction guide](reproducing.md) for the associated experiments and settings.
